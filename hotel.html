About this sanctuary

Concealed at the base of Kyoto’s forested eastern mountains, The Cedar Sanctuary is a masterwork of sustainable Japanese carpentry and acoustic tranquility.

Constructed using salvaged Yoshino cedar wood and traditional joinery without metal nails, the hotel merges contemporary comforts with the timeless rituals of Japanese bathing. Guests awaken to the gentle chimes of nearby Nanzen-ji temple, followed by handcrafted breakfasts made from organic produce grown in the Kameoka valley.

Each suite is acoustically treated to preserve quiet reflection, featuring private open-air hinoki wood tubs, woven tatami rest zones, and custom natural-fiber bedding designed for deep, restorative sleep.
